What you need

Four things, two are free

1
Claude desktop app, Code tab

On the Pro plan or above. The Code tab can install tools, save files and render video, which normal chat can't.

2
The hormozi-hooks skill

Free and open source. It writes hooks using Alex Hormozi's hooks x bodies x CTAs system.

3
HyperFrames

Free and open source from HeyGen. It turns HTML into video on your own laptop. Motion ads and hook stamps cost nothing.

4
Higgsfield, either way

A paid plan connects through the Higgsfield MCP. No plan? A free API key and a $10 top-up, then you pay per ad.

Honest bit: it drafts, you approve. Nothing spends money, pauses an ad or moves budget without your tap.

00

Setup, once

Install the two free tools

Open the Code tab, paste this, and let Claude do the installing.

Connect Higgsfield: pick one route

No subscription (my pick). Go to console.higgsfield.ai, create an API key (it's only shown once, so copy it), top up $10, then paste Prompt 00b. You pay per ad and spending stops at zero.

Already on a paid plan? In Claude, go to Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Paste https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp and log in.

Connect your ads account (optional, for step 3)

In Claude: Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Paste https://mcp.facebook.com/ads and log in with Facebook. Meta's official connector is free in open beta and still rolling out, so if you don't see it yet, steps 1 and 2 work without it.

01

Step 1

The Copywriter

Most brands write one hook per ad. This writes 20, spread across Eugene Schwartz's 5 levels of awareness, from people who don't know they have the problem to people already buying from a rival.

Why 100 is honest: it's 20 real videos with 5 different openings each. Same body, new first 2 seconds. The hook stamps are free because HyperFrames runs on your laptop.

02

Step 2

The Studio

One product photo becomes real-looking creator ads. Selling software? Claude makes motion graphics ads with HyperFrames instead.

  • The Virality Predictor scores each ad's hook and attention before you spend. In my test the fancy motion ad scored lowest (43) and both UGC ads peaked at second 12, which meant the hook came too late.
  • Ask for 1 or 2 ads per prompt, not 20. Check them, then make more of what works.
  • Higgsfield templates (Marketing Studio presets) are a quick win for product shots. Ask Claude to show you a few first.
03

Step 3 · my favourite

The Approve button

Every two days Claude pulls the numbers and drafts one card: double down on these, pause these, remix the winner into five new hooks. You reply APPROVE or you don't.

  1. Paste Prompt 03 into the Claude desktop app and say "schedule this every 2 days at 9am".
  2. Claude sets it up as a scheduled task. You can see and edit it in the app any time.
  3. When the card lands, read it, then reply APPROVE (or tell it what to change).

Honest note: scheduled tasks only run while your laptop is awake and Claude is open. If it's asleep, the run happens when it wakes up.
